From Scattered Chats to One Summary

A case-summarisation concept that condenses prior employee conversations, case notes and actions into one brief at the moment an HR agent opens a related request.

OVERVIEW

What the experience is designed to do.

A case-summarisation concept that condenses prior employee conversations, case notes and actions into one brief at the moment an HR agent opens a related request.

THE CHALLENGE

Why this matters.

When an employee has contacted HR multiple times about a connected issue, a new case owner can spend time reconstructing context before helping. The opportunity is to surface the important history immediately.

DESIGN APPROACH

How I would structure it.

  • Define a useful summary structure: issue, prior resolution attempts, outstanding actions and urgency.
  • Pull together only the relevant prior threads or case notes linked to the employee and issue.
  • Present the summary as the first view on case open rather than an optional extra step.
  • Keep traceability back to source records so the agent can validate details when necessary.
