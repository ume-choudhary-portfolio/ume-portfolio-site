OVERVIEW

What the experience is designed to do.

An AI service concept for employees on international assignment, covering tax equalisation, visa status and relocation policy with country-aware guidance and specialist escalation.

THE CHALLENGE

Why this matters.

Global Mobility queries can be urgent but often repetitive. Employees need fast policy guidance, while assignment-specific or country-specific exceptions still require a specialist.

DESIGN APPROACH

How I would structure it.

  • Identify recurring Global Mobility query types from available case history or service-owner workshops.
  • Structure knowledge around assignment type and country-specific policy where appropriate.
  • Design the agent to distinguish policy questions from assignment-specific exceptions.
  • Trigger human escalation when an answer requires specialist judgement, sensitive personal data or country-specific advice.
