OVERVIEW

One programme, multiple workstreams.

The programme connected people-process requirements across Talent Acquisition, Performance Management, Rewards, Global Mobility, Payroll and Legal. The work combined service design, requirements, governance, Workday integration dependencies, readiness and stakeholder decision support.

THE CHALLENGE

Consistency across a global footprint.

Regional and functional variation created a need to align local operating realities with a common transformation model, while managing dependencies and risks across multiple workstreams and integrations.

HOW I ACHIEVED IT

Discovery → design → governance → readiness.

  • Ran discovery workshops across workstreams to surface pain points, operating variations and people-process requirements.
  • Converted findings into process flows, functional requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria and implementation roadmaps.
  • Owned RAID governance so delivery risks and decisions were visible across workstreams.
  • Coordinated Workday integration dependencies and go-live readiness across the programme.
  • Created and maintained a project and delivery health dashboard covering sprint velocity, backlog burn down, defect ageing, RAID status and go live readiness, giving programme leadership a single decision ready view.
  • Presented HRSD, EmployeeWorks, Employee Slate and conversational employee-intake capabilities to senior stakeholders and C-suite executives.
