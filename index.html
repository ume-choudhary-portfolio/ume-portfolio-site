About me

From operational problems to enterprise transformation.

My career has been built around one recurring challenge: understanding what the business actually needs, then turning that need into a solution that can be designed, built, adopted and measured.

I began by digitising HR, legal and compliance operations, which gave me a practical grounding in process, controls and user needs. At Amazon, that expanded into enterprise workflow automation across IT, Facilities, Legal, Compliance and HR. At CT Global, I led multi-function ServiceNow delivery across ITSM, HR, Legal, Audit and Finance. I now work at Capgemini Invent, leading discovery and functional delivery for a global ServiceNow transformation spanning multiple HR and service workstreams.

My strongest platform specialism is ServiceNow, with particular interest in HR transformation and employee services. I work comfortably across business and technical teams — from C-suite capability conversations and discovery workshops through user stories, process design, UAT, go-live readiness and post-launch improvement.
