About me

From hands-on HR operations to global people transformation.

My career has been built around improving how people experience HR and operational services — understanding what employees, managers and business leaders need, then turning those needs into practical processes, controls, service models and measurable improvements.

I began in HR and legal operations, digitising manual processes and building practical governance, escalation and reporting structures. At Amazon, that expanded into high-volume cross-functional operations across HR, Facilities, Legal, Compliance and IT. At CT Global, I led people-service and enterprise workflow transformation across multiple functions. I now work at Capgemini Invent, leading HR service transformation within a global programme spanning 70+ countries and 10+ integrations.

My focus is People Operations and HR transformation, with ServiceNow as my strongest enabling platform. I partner with stakeholders across Talent Acquisition, Performance Management, Rewards, Global Mobility and Payroll, while working across business and technical teams from discovery and process design through UAT, go-live readiness, adoption and continuous improvement.
