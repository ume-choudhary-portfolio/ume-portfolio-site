Dashboard & KPI reporting

Five reporting views that turn delivery data into decisions.

I use dashboards to make project health, process performance, adoption, risk and Agile delivery visible. The objective is not reporting for reporting's sake. It is to give Product Owners, programme leads and senior stakeholders a clear view of what needs attention, what is improving and what decision is required next.

01

PROJECT & DELIVERY HEALTH

Project & Delivery Health Dashboard

A leadership view designed to show whether delivery is genuinely healthy before a steering committee meeting rather than relying on a manual status deck.

What it tracks

  • Sprint velocity and throughput trends
  • Backlog burn down and epic progress
  • Defect ageing and unresolved release blockers
  • RAID status, dependencies and go live readiness

How it is used

  • Flags workstreams drifting off plan
  • Highlights risks that require leadership decisions
  • Creates one common view before governance forums
  • Reduces time spent manually reconciling project health
Velocity delivery trendDefect ageing release riskReadiness go live view
02

PROCESS TRANSFORMATION KPI

KYC & Onboarding KPI Dashboard

A process-performance view that makes the impact of journey redesign visible from identity verification through screening and EDD.

What it tracks

  • Case volume by onboarding stage
  • Average handling and cycle time
  • Stage by stage drop off and bottlenecks
  • Handoffs across verification, screening and EDD

Why it matters

  • Makes the 8 to 5 handoff redesign visible
  • Shows approximately 20% cycle time improvement
  • Helps teams identify where work queues build up
  • Gives stakeholders a common baseline for improvement
8 → 5 handoffs~20% cycle timeStage view bottlenecks
03

ADOPTION & USAGE

Tool Adoption & Usage Dashboard

A view built to answer a simple question after rollout: are people actually using the tool in the way the transformation expected?

What it tracks

  • Active user rates
  • Ticket or story throughput
  • Training completion
  • Usage by team, workstream or module

How it supports adoption

  • Identifies teams where adoption is lagging
  • Targets follow up enablement sessions
  • Separates training completion from real usage
  • Provides evidence for post go live support decisions
Users active rateTraining completionThroughput real usage
04

RAID & RISK HEALTH

RAID & Risk Health Dashboard

A live governance view that turns risks, actions, issues and dependencies into a decision ready heat map rather than a static spreadsheet.

What it tracks

  • Open risks and current severity
  • Issues requiring escalation
  • Cross team dependencies and owners
  • Actions, due dates and overdue items

How it is used

  • Creates visibility before governance meetings
  • Connects Confluence reporting with live Jira items
  • Shows ownership and ageing clearly
  • Reduces manual leadership status preparation
On trackWatchAt riskDependency
05

SPRINT & BACKLOG HEALTH

Sprint & Backlog Health Dashboard

A Jira based Agile delivery view that translates backlog mechanics into a language both technical and non technical stakeholders can use for decisions.

What it tracks

  • Burndown and remaining scope
  • Sprint velocity
  • Cumulative flow and work in progress
  • Epic progress and cross team dependencies

How it supports delivery

  • Used during sprint planning and reviews
  • Helps Product Owners challenge unrealistic commitments
  • Surfaces blockers and work ageing
  • Makes delivery health understandable outside the scrum team
