AI Virtual Agent for Payroll

OVERVIEW

What the experience is designed to do.

An AI agent concept for real-time payslip and deduction questions, with a clear privacy boundary between general payroll policy and employee-specific record queries.

THE CHALLENGE

Why this matters.

Payroll enquiries spike around pay cycles and employees often need fast answers. The design challenge is to resolve simple policy questions quickly without weakening privacy or access controls.

DESIGN APPROACH

How I would structure it.

  • Map payroll query types and classify them by sensitivity: general policy vs individual record questions.
  • Define which query types can be answered directly and which require secure hand-off.
  • Explain deductions in plain language with a reference to the relevant approved policy or payroll rule.
  • Keep employee-specific record access behind appropriate authentication and human escalation where required.
